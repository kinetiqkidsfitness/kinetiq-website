A full year, mapped out

Six modules. One movement-literate kid.

Each module runs seven weeks plus a Movement IQ Showcase — building from foundational patterns to real athletic confidence.

1 · Movement Foundations

Squat, push, hinge, pull, lunge, rotate, carry — the seven patterns everything else builds on.

How does my body move?

2 · Agility & Coordination

Quick feet, acceleration, deceleration, and change of direction.

Can I control my speed?

3 · Power Development

Jump, land, throw, and push or pull with real explosiveness.

How strong can I become?

4 · Balance & Control

Static and dynamic balance, core stability, body awareness.

How do I control my body?

5 · Engine & Endurance

Steady movement, carries, obstacles, and team-built resilience.

How do I keep moving?

6 · Athletic Movement & Mobility

Ground movement, transitions, and flow — moving with confidence in every direction.

How do I move with confidence?
