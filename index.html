Why Movement Literacy Matters

Movement Is Learned.

Just like reading or math, movement is a skill children build through practice. Before kids can run fast, play a sport, or stay injury-free, they need to master how their body moves.

Squat Hinge Push Pull Carry Crawl Rotate Lunge

These eight patterns become the foundation for sports, injury prevention, and a lifetime of movement confidence.
