02 · What you doAt home

You don’t need to know how to program exercise.

Your job is the part only you can do: showing up, cheering, and noticing how they did.

Before classMake a little space.Clear a spot on the floor and open today's class. The app lists any equipment, like a beanbag or a cone.
During classCheer them on.Coach Char does the teaching. You encourage effort and help when needed.
After classCheck in.Answer a quick check-in about how it went. That's how progress gets recorded.
