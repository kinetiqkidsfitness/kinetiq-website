Who coaches KinetIQ

Built for the adults kids already trust.

TeachersBring structured movement into classrooms and school programs without designing workouts.
After-school staffGive staff a repeatable physical-literacy curriculum they can lead with confidence.
Youth & rec coachesBuild the fundamental movement skills that carry into every sport and activity.
