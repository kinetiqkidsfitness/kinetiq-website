About KinetIQ

Confidence follows competence.

A kid who seems “not sporty” usually just hasn’t been taught the basics yet. When kids learn how to move well, they start to believe they can.

01 · What that means

So we built a curriculum, not a workout.

Typical workout content Something to do. Choose a video, follow along, finish. Tomorrow starts from zero.
KinetIQ Something to build on. Every class builds on the last, so each skill a kid earns makes the next one easier.
