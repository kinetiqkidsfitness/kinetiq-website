The KinetIQ curriculum

A real curriculum that grows with them.

Not a playlist of workouts. KinetIQ teaches movement in order, builds the whole kid, and keeps going as they grow.

1Progressive, not randomSkills are taught in order, and each class builds on the last.
2The whole kidStrength and movement, plus balance, focus, control and grit.
3Grows with themA pathway of modules that keeps challenging kids as they get more capable.
The KinetIQ method

2 classes. 1 skill. Every week.

Class ALearnLearn the week’s new movement skill.
Class BApplyPut the new skill to work alongside movements they’ve already learned.
Every weekNothing resets.Each new skill builds on the ones before it.
